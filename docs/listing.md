# Store listing (CurseForge)

## Name

Post Rider

## Summary (short description)

Mailboxes, letters and parcels, carried across your world by twenty mounted post riders, who don't always make it.

## Categories / tags

Adventure, Transportation, Utility, Mobs, Storage. NeoForge, 26.3 and 1.21.1, client and server.

## Description

**Post your mail. Watch it ride away.**

Put up a mailbox, give it an address, and post a parcel or a letter to any other mailbox in your world. One of
twenty post riders appears in a puff of smoke at your box, takes the mail, whistles up a horse and rides off across
country with it. Usually it arrives.

### Meet the crew

There are twenty riders, and each is always the same person, with their own face, outfit and horse. Chris rides
Lightning, the fastest horse on the road. Gus Tanner plods along on Molasses. "One-Eyed" Nell Harper wears her
eyepatch, and Mattie Cole's red braid flies behind her. You'll get to know them: "Chris is here!"

### A real journey

Riders really ride the whole way, over hills, around ravines and across rivers by ferry barge. At dusk they pick a
flat pitch, hammer in the tent pegs, strike up a campfire with a flint and steel and play the harmonica until the
monsters come out. Then they turn in, boots
sticking out of the tent door, and ride on at 6am. Along the way they wave at you, tip their hats to villagers, swat
flies in the swamp and wipe their brows in the desert.

### Dangers of the road

Zombies, skeletons, spiders by night, drowned with tridents and guardians will all go for a rider (and creepers go off if he rides too close), and so can players.
Riders carry a couple of healing potions, but not every delivery survives. Lose one and you'll find a **Lost Parcel**
where it happened: a skull in a cowboy hat beside the mail, a crumpled map if the rider got lost, or a soggy wreck
bobbing on the water. Only the sender, the addressee, or whoever did the deed can open it. The sender gets a letter
telling them what happened and where.

### Features

- Mailboxes in all 16 colours, with a 27-delivery inbox, a raising flag and an old-time post office screen.
- Letters you write and sign, with postmarks, and parcels of up to 9 stacks.
- 20 unique riders, each with their own horse, plus 36 animations: riding, camping, rowing, drinking, lighting the
  campfire, studying the map and 20 little mannerisms.
- Real routes: riders plan the whole trip on a paper map, ride round bays and ravines, use tunnels, climb ladders on
  foot, leap gaps and only take the ferry barge when the way round is too far.
- Campfires that light up the camp through the evening and burn down to embers by 3am.
- Lost Parcels and failure letters when things go wrong.
- Fifteen advancements on their own tab, one of them secret.
- Server settings for rider count, route planning range, potions, barge speed and collection hours.
- No other mods needed.

*Needs NeoForge 26.3 for Minecraft 26.3, or NeoForge 21.1 for Minecraft 1.21.1 (a separate download), on both the server
and clients. No other mods needed; the Concurrent Chunk Management Engine (C2ME) is recommended where there's a build
for your version.*
